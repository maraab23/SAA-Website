Join the Sequence Analysis Association

Why join?

Membership in the SAA is open to everyone interested in sequence analysis and related methods — researchers, teachers, students and practitioners. As a member you

  • have the right to vote at the SAA General Assembly (see the statutes),
  • receive the newsletter with news about SAA activities, webinars and events,
  • help shape the association’s events, webinars and training activities.

How to join

  • Read the SAA statutes — they describe the structure, purposes and functioning of the association.
  • Fill in the membership request form below. Please tell us briefly about your interest in sequence analysis.
  • The Executive Committee reviews your request; this may take a few days. You will receive an email once it has been approved.
